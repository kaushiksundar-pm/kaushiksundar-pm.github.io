Product Case Study — AI & Platform Infrastructure

AI-Powered Scientific Research Assistant

Role: Technical Product Manager Stack: GPT, Claude, Gemini, Multi-Model Orchestration, LangChain, Vector Databases, Kubernetes

⚠️ The Problem & Context

Researchers and scientists spent weeks manually parsing thousands of unstructured scientific publications, laboratory notebooks, and dense biochemical datasets. Finding, extracting, and cross-referencing information was slow, highly siloed, and prone to human error.

The Technical Hurdles
  • Hallucination Risks: In scientific and clinical environments, unsupported LLM responses can invalidate research and lead to costly laboratory workflow errors.
  • Lack of Provenance: Standard generative AI systems do not inherently track the precise source or original coordinates of extracted facts.
  • Data Silos: Scientific content arrived in fragmented formats, including PDFs, proprietary instrument files, and genomic sequences that traditional pipelines could not process consistently.

🛠️ Product Strategy & Discovery

Led the discovery phase through interviews with lead scientists to identify critical friction points. Rather than building a generic wrapper around a single AI model, shaped a multi-tenant orchestration platform that converts unstructured documents into provenance-aware, structured scientific assets.

Key Product Trade-offs
  • Multi-LLM Strategy vs. Single Model Vendor: Designed a dynamic workflow using GPT, Claude, and Gemini because different models offered distinct strengths in semantic reasoning, large-table processing, cost efficiency, and performance.
  • Proactive Constraints vs. Passive Filtering: Prioritized structured templates and validated schemas over unrestricted free-form prompts so generated outputs could conform to scientific data requirements.

🏗️ Platform Architecture & Pillars

1. Dynamic RAG Pipeline
Shaped an enterprise Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline covering document ingestion, intelligent text chunking, semantic metadata tagging, and specialized knowledge-graph indexing.
2. Multi-Model Orchestration and Structured Outputs
Designed an extraction layer that converts raw scientific text into structured JSON and Pydantic-compatible schemas, supported by programmatic validation against predefined scientific ontologies.
3. Traceable Citation Provenance
Established metadata tracking so generated insights could be linked to their source document, page number, and bounding-box coordinates for rapid scientist verification.
4. Human-in-the-Loop Validation
Implemented review workflows requiring credentialed scientists to validate AI-generated conclusions before they entered downstream laboratory processes.

🚀 Execution & Operating Model

  • Spec-Driven Development: Introduced a Markdown-based specification framework that clarified end-to-end user journeys and exposed API and platform dependencies earlier in delivery.
  • Pod and Squad Governance: Led four cross-functional delivery teams comprising approximately 25 data scientists, machine-learning engineers, product specialists, and UX professionals.

📈 Measured Value Realization

  • Research Velocity: Reduced scientific data extraction and synthesis workflows from weeks to hours, helping research teams evaluate and adjust discovery strategies more quickly.
  • Trust and Auditability: Improved governance through provenance-aware processing, structured validation, traceable citations, and human approval controls.
  • Platform Reusability: Designed reusable extraction services with structured REST API contracts so additional digital chemistry products could consume the platform capabilities.
